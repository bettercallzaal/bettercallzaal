## 1. Your site, in your name

For a small business, an artist or a collective that wants a simple website they own outright and can change without calling anyone.

**You get**
- A small site, up to about five pages.
- Your own domain, GitHub and hosting accounts, opened in your name and your email.
- The site moved into those accounts.
- One sitting, about 75 minutes, where you change a line on your own site and undo it, by your own hand.
- A one-page guide and a checklist to keep.

**Not included:** shops, logins, databases, booking engines, or logo and brand design. If your site sells something, hosting has a monthly cost that is yours, and I tell you what it is before we start.

**How long:** about two weeks of build, then the handover sitting.

**Example:** [northcreekme.com](https://northcreekme.com), the site of North Creek, a music collective in Maine. The handover steps above were written for that site in October 2026.

**Already have a site that someone else holds?** I do the handover on its own.

## 2. One AI agent workflow, set up and taught

For a small team with one job that repeats every week and eats someone's time.

**You get**
- One workflow, picked together on a first call.
- It runs in your accounts, with Claude.
- A written rulebook for it, in plain words.
- A person approves anything that sends, posts or spends. The agent never does those alone.
- One teaching session, and two weeks of fixes after.

**Not included:** a second workflow, anything that acts with no human approval, or replacing software you already use.

**How long:** about three weeks from the first call.

**Example:** my own work runs this way every day. The rules are in [How I work with AI agents](README.md#how-i-work-with-ai-agents), and [zorca](https://github.com/bettercallzaal/zorca), the layer that organizes the agents, is a public repo.
